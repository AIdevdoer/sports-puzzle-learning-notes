Design notes for a 38-0 Premier League season challenge

A perfect league season sounds simple: win every match. The design problem is harder because a Premier League schedule contains 38 rounds, changing opponents, and repeated recovery decisions.

What the puzzle should measure

The challenge should reward consistency rather than one strong starting lineup. A useful model must track fatigue, squad depth, and the cost of using the same players every week.

Why rotation matters

Each selection should affect the next match. Resting a key player can reduce the chance of winning today, but it can improve the squad for a difficult fixture later. That trade-off gives the season structure meaning.

A playable reference

The 38-0 Premier League season challenge turns this idea into a browser game. The goal is to manage the whole campaign and finish with 38 wins from 38 league matches.

Useful checks for similar games

  • Make the current round and season record visible.
  • Explain why a lineup decision changes the expected result.
  • Keep the match loop short enough for mobile play.
  • Show failure as part of the season, not as a hidden reset.
